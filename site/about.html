About

Hello, I’m
Shraddha.

Teacher, student, and a lifelong believer in the quiet power of breath and stillness.

Practice, with devotion.

My name, Shraddha, means faith — a quiet, steady trust. It’s the same word Patañjali uses for the very first thing a yogi brings to the mat. I didn’t choose my name, but I’ve spent my life trying to live up to it.

I named this home Sādhana — a daily, devoted practice. Put the two words together and you have everything I believe in a single breath: practice, with devotion.

Yoga found me not in strength but in a season of needing to slow down. It taught me that showing up gently, again and again, is its own kind of strength. That’s the yoga I teach and the yoga I’m still learning.

“You do not need to be flexible. You do not need to be calm. You only need to be willing to begin.”

— Shraddha
